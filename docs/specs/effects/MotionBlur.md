# Effect spec: `MotionBlur`

Blurs the layer along its own on-canvas motion, which comes from
keyframed or behaviour-driven transforms.

Tags: **[obs]** observed in files, **[inf]** inferred, **[?]** unknown.
For the shared evidence see `README.md`.

## Evidence

- **MotionBlurPreview.** A 512×512 image at scale 0.2. Its `translation0`
  goes from −465 to 59 to −55 to 458 and its `rotation2` goes from 0 to
  −2π, with `bnce` and `eioe` interpolation. It carries
  `MotionBlur {intensity: 1.0}`. Project metadata has
  `EffectPreviewTime: 2.8` [obs].
- **MediaMattePreview.** `MotionBlur` with `intensity` keyframed from 1.0 to
  2.0 over 30 frames, on a clip that also has a fly-out behaviour [obs].

## Parameters

| Name | Type | Values seen | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `intensity` | animatable double (constant in format 4.0 files) | 1.0 (default), 2.0 | Blur strength, as a multiple of the default shutter [inf]. 0 = off |

That is the only parameter [obs].

## Model (provisional)

```
for k in 0..N−1:   s_k = t + (k/(N−1) − 0.5) · shutter · intensity
                    draw the layer with its transform evaluated at s_k, weight 1/N
```

- `shutter` = one output frame (1 / `videoFormatFrameRate`) at intensity 1
  [inf, weak]. The window could also trail the time instead of being
  centred on it [?].
- Only **transform motion** blurs: translation, scale, rotation, and
  behaviour motion [inf]. Pixel changes inside a video do not.
- A static layer is unchanged [inf].

## EF6: motion-blur check (generated by qa)

This needs a **video export**, not a still: export `ef6.mp4` at 1920×1080,
30 fps.

- **Clip A:** M-grey at scale 0.25, `translation0` keyframed linearly from
  −800 to +800 over exactly 1 s, so it moves 53.33 px per frame.
- **Clips B–D:** three copies on other rows with `intensity` 0.5, 1 and 2.
- **Clip E:** a fifth row with a rotation-only animation, 0 → 2π in 1 s.

The streak length per frame divided by 53.33 gives the shutter fraction.
