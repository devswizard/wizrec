## Download

Download **WizRec-*.dmg** below, open it and drag **WizRec** into **Applications**.
Nothing else to install: FFmpeg and everything WizRec needs are inside the app.

Requirements: a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

### First launch

WizRec is free and not notarized by Apple, so macOS asks once before opening it:

1. Open **Applications**, right-click **WizRec** and choose **Open**.
2. Click **Open** in the dialog.

If macOS only offers *Move to Trash*: open **System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security**,
scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to WizRec.

### Permissions

To record, allow WizRec under **System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security** for
**Screen & System Audio Recording**, **Camera** and **Microphone** when macOS asks.
